DATA POLICY
- Normalized card terms come from current official issuer pages.
- Automated crawling remains installed as a discovery and update-checking tool.
- The crawler does not automatically become the primary financial ranking.
- Cards are added to the verified ranking only when enough source data exists to model the economics.
- The ranking is limited to the currently normalized CredoNomics dataset and does not claim to include every issued card in India.

CHANGE CONTROL
- Some issuer pages announce future-effective terms. V10 models terms effective on the database checked date, not future terms before their effective date.
