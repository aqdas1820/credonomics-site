CREDONOMICS V13 — ATELIER FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE UI
=====================================================

DESIGN DIRECTION
The redesign is a distinct CredoNomics system informed by current best practices seen
across high-quality financial/product websites, without copying their proprietary
layouts, imagery, branding, or assets.

Influence translated into CredoNomics:
- Stripe: decisive hero hierarchy + controlled gradient energy
- Linear: calmer product chrome, lower visual noise, consistent controls
- Ramp / Brex: finance-first dashboard confidence and strong numeric hierarchy
- Mercury: whitespace and restrained premium surfaces
- Wise: direct language, simple calls to action, readable scale
- Morningstar: precision, research clarity, useful data hierarchy
- FinChat: financial-terminal feel without overwhelming the user
