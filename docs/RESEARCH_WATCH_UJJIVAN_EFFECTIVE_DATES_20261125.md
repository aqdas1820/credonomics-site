### Ujjivan Small Finance Bank — retail FD/RD schedule effective 19 November

New data-driven article:
`/research/watch/ujjivan-sfb-fd-rd-rates-effective-19-november-2026`

The bank's official interest-rates page states a published date of 19 November 2026 and says the listed domestic FD, NRO, NRE, RD, Platina and Tax Saver schedules are effective from 19 November. The page does not state a separate announcement date.

- Domestic FDs below ₹3 crore: 8.10% p.a. for 3 years 1 day–3 years 6 months; eligible resident senior citizens receive an additional 0.50%.
- NRO: same 8.10% tenor rate; no senior-citizen addition.
- NRE: 8.10% for the same tenor; minimum one-year tenure; the bank states no interest and no penalty on closure within one year, and no interest on the amount partially withdrawn within one year.
- Recurring deposits: 8.10% for 39–42 months; 0.50% senior addition for an eligible resident senior citizen as primary holder.
- Platina: 8.20% for 3 years 1 day–3 years 6 months, but only for deposits above ₹1 crore and below ₹3 crore; non-callable, no part/pre-closure, no auto-renewal and no senior addition.
- Tax Saver FD: 7.20% for five years.
- Rates are subject to change; the bank says the applicable rate depends on the date and time funds are received. No prior-rate comparison or deposit recommendation is asserted.

Primary source: https://www.ujjivansfb.bank.in/interest-rates

Access note: the official page's direct fetch returned HTTP 403 in this review, but the official-domain search result returned the full page text and rate tables. The associated support PDF is marked “Internal document”; it is not linked or presented as a public notice.
