# Research Watch evidence — Flipkart Wholesale HDFC Bank Credit Card

## Editorial decision

Publish a focused, cardholder-only guide. HDFC Bank's current official product page contains a specific Important Information notice: the card is being phased out, existing customers are being migrated to another HDFC Bank credit card, cashback stops on spends from 1 December 2026, and eligible November 2026 cashback is due in the first week of December.

This is new to the current Research Watch dataset and was not found in the existing hub or November bulletin. It is not a card-ranking update.

## Source and verification

- Primary source: https://www.hdfc.bank.in/business-credit-cards/flipkart-wholesale-credit-card
- Issuer: HDFC Bank.
- Checked: 20 November 2026.
- Customer communication date stated by HDFC: 31 October 2026.
- Effective date for cashback cessation: 1 December 2026.
- Expected timing for eligible November cashback: first week of December 2026.
- Source location: the page's Important Information block. The same page continues to display legacy cashback benefit bullets lower down; the dated Important Information notice is treated as controlling for the stated 1 December cutoff.

## Confirmed points

1. HDFC says the card is being phased out and existing customers are being migrated to another HDFC Bank credit card.
2. HDFC says migration communications were sent to existing customers on 31 October 2026 and asks them to check SMS/email for details.
3. Spends on the Flipkart Wholesale HDFC Bank Credit Card will not earn cashback from 1 December 2026.
4. Eligible cashback for November 2026 spends is stated to be credited in the first week of December 2026.

## Explicit HOLD / limitations

- No separate public announcement date is stated; 31 October is the customer-communication date stated by HDFC.
- The public page does not identify each customer's replacement variant or individual migration date.
- No replacement-card fee, rewards, limit, consent/choice process, old-card end date, or recurring-mandate transfer rule is stated.
- The page does not resolve transactions made before 1 December but posted after the cutoff, individual exclusions, reversals, or a separate cashback claim process.
- The older cashback-benefit bullets and application sections remain lower on the same page. Do not apply the old cashback benefits to spends from 1 December onward; do not infer whether new applications remain open.
- Do not infer automatic migration details, refund eligibility, or replacement-card benefits.
