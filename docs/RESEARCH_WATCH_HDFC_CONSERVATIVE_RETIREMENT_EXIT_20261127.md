# Research Watch release note — HDFC Conservative Hybrid and Retirement Fund covered-call change

## Editorial decision

Add one independently checked guide for HDFC Mutual Fund's 18 November 2026 Notice-cum-Addendum No. 1378. It introduces a covered-call strategy in HDFC Conservative Hybrid Fund and HDFC Retirement Fund — Equity Plan, Hybrid-Equity Plan and Hybrid-Debt Plan, effective 30 December 2026, and gives a defined optional exit window to a specific group of existing unitholders.

This is distinct from the 24 October guide for HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund and HDFC Children's Fund. No existing article is overwritten and no fund ranking or portfolio formula is changed.

## Dates kept distinct

- Notice / announcement date: 18 November 2026.
- Earlier SEBI email noted in the notice: 25 September 2026; the notice says SEBI noted the proposal, not that SEBI approved or recommended it.
- Eligible applications: valid applications received by the Fund up to 3:00 p.m. on 18 November 2026.
- Optional exit window: 30 November 2026 through 3:00 p.m. on 29 December 2026, both dates inclusive.
- Covered-call change effective date: 30 December 2026.
- Independent recheck: 27 November 2026.

## Verified details retained

- Affected schemes: HDFC Conservative Hybrid Fund and HDFC Retirement Fund — Equity Plan, Hybrid-Equity Plan and Hybrid-Debt Plan.
- The notice treats the covered-call permission as a change in fundamental attributes.
- Covered calls are limited to constituent stocks of NIFTY 50 and BSE SENSEX.
- Total notional value of written calls, taking into account strike price and premium value, cannot exceed 15% of the total market value of the underlying equity shares held by the scheme at all times.
- Shares underlying written calls cannot exceed 30% of the unencumbered shares of the particular company held by the scheme at all times.
- A call cannot be written without holding the underlying equity shares; the scheme cannot enter into another derivative contract to hedge a written call; the option is marked to market daily.
- The notice says option premium may generate additional income, but the downside from a fall in the underlying share price is reduced only to the extent of the premium earned.
- Eligible existing holders may switch to another HDFC Mutual Fund scheme or redeem at applicable NAV without exit load during the stated optional window.
- The exit option is voluntary. If a holder has no objection, no action is required and consent is deemed.
- Retirement Fund exit is subject to the applicable SID lock-in. Pledged/encumbered units require a release letter; frozen/locked folios require the freeze to be vacated or the relevant legal compliance to be completed.
- SIP cancellation is separate if future instalments are not wanted. The notice asks holders to update address/payout-bank details at least 10 working days before exercising the option; demat holders should contact their DP.
- The notice says the AMC board approved the changes, trustees noted them and SEBI noted the proposal by email. It is not described as SEBI approval or endorsement.

## Explicitly not claimed

- The optional exit window is not for every holder; it is limited to valid applications received by the Fund up to 3:00 p.m. on 18 November.
- No guarantee of option premium, improved returns, capital protection or full downside offset.
- No exact Retirement Fund lock-in duration is inferred; the applicable SID governs.
- No exit eligibility is inferred for pledged, encumbered or frozen units beyond the notice's express conditions.
- No tax outcome is calculated.
- No ranking, scheme suitability score, portfolio record or investment formula is changed.
