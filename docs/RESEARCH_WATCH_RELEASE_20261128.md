## Published updates

### 1. RBI final KYC Amendment Directions: suspected money-mule debit-hold SOP

- **Affected audience:** Customers of commercial banks (including small finance banks, payments banks, local area banks and regional rural banks) and urban co-operative banks.
- **Announcement date:** 27 November 2026.
- **Default commencement:** 1 July 2027; RBI says an individual bank may implement earlier.
- **Verified scope:** RBI's press release says the six final KYC Amendment Directions address a bank SOP for temporary debit holds on amounts or accounts suspected to be linked to money-mule activity and cyber-enabled financial fraud, and removal of such holds in coordination with Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs). RBI says feedback on the September draft was examined and modifications incorporated.
- **Consumer action:** For a restriction applied now, ask the bank for the written reason and the current bank, statutory or LEA instruction relied on. Do not assume the future 2027 SOP governs a present restriction. Keep ordinary records for legitimate receipts and use only the bank's verified official channel.
- **Limitations:** The final notification attachment text could not be independently extracted during this check. No final transaction threshold, customer reply window, bank decision deadline, maximum hold duration, account-level-hold test or complaint-resolution deadline is asserted. The September draft's ₹1,000 threshold, 20-day explanation period, 60-day maximum hold and 1 April 2027 proposed start are not presented as final terms. No claim is made that every suspicion causes a whole-account hold or that an existing restriction is automatically lifted.
