## Verified product details recorded

- KIM version 3 dated 27 October 2026 lists the NFO from 17 November through 1 December 2026 and continuous sale/repurchase reopening on 10 December 2026.
- Open-ended equity long-short SIF; Nifty 100 TRI benchmark; risk band 3 for the strategy and benchmark at NFO stage, subject to reassessment after actual holdings.
- Indicative allocation: 80–100% equity/equity-related instruments, 0–20% debt/money-market instruments and 0–20% InvITs. Unhedged short exposure through equity derivatives may reach 25% of net assets.
- Ordinary investor threshold: ₹10 lakh aggregate across Infinity SIF strategies at PAN level; regular Kotak mutual-fund schemes do not count. KIM lists ₹1 lakh for accredited investors and limited exceptions for specified existing SIF investors and mandatory AMC designated-employee investments.
- Daily subscription/redemption on business days; partial redemptions are constrained by the aggregate ₹10 lakh threshold. If market-value changes passively take the aggregate below that threshold, KIM permits only full redemption of the remaining SIF investment.
- SIP/SWP/STP commence only after reopening; KIM lists ₹10,000 per instalment and at least six instalments, still subject to the initial aggregate SIF threshold.
- Exit load: 0.50% for redemption/switch-out on or before completion of 30 days from allotment; nil thereafter, with stated IDCW-reinvestment and plan/option-switch exceptions.
- KIM expense schedule: maximum base expense ratio of 2.10% p.a. for the first ₹500 crore AUM slab, with lower ceilings for higher slabs; brokerage, transaction costs and statutory levies may be additional. This is not represented as an all-in current TER.
- New strategy; portfolio holdings/turnover history not available in the KIM. Capital loss, liquidity and market risks remain. KIM says SEBI has neither approved nor disapproved the units nor certified the KIM; no investment-objective assurance is stated.
- Kotak terms are explicitly distinguished from the separate HDFC H-SIF interval strategy; HDFC's redemption schedule and 90-day exit-load terms are not applied to Kotak.
