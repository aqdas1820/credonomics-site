The original Amex guide now includes the official Payments & Charges notice. From 15 November, payments made after 9 pm appear in the card account the next day. The notice gives no separate announcement date. This posting change is distinct from the temporary 12–16 November display delay. Cardholders should allow processing time for their chosen channel and verify that payment appears on the account.
