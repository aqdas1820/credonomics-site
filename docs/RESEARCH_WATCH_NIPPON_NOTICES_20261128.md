## 1. Nippon India: IDCW-option fresh-subscription suspension

- **Affected audience:** Investors in the IDCW option of the 28 schemes specifically named in the notice.
- **Notice / announcement date:** 25 November 2026 (Notice Cum Addendum No. 89).
- **Checked:** 28 November 2026.
- **Effective date:** 2 December 2026.
- **Verified scope:** Fresh and additional lump sums, switch-ins, new SIP/STP registrations, and SIP amount modification or Step-Up requests are not accepted in the IDCW option of the listed schemes until further notice. Transactions received and time-stamped by 3:00 pm on 1 December are to be accepted at applicable NAV. Existing SIPs and STPs registered before the effective date continue. Regulatory-mandated AMC/designated-employee contributions are exempt.
- **Limitations:** The notice is option- and scheme-specific. It does not announce a blanket suspension of every Nippon India scheme or every plan option. It does not state a separate rule for existing SWPs. No redemption, refund, tax or return outcome is inferred.

Primary sources:
- Notice Cum Addendum No. 89 (25 November 2026): https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/InvestorServices/Addenda/suspension-of-subscription-in-IDCW-final.pdf
- Official Notice/Addendum register: https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/investor-service/quick-links/notice-addendum/

## 2. Nippon India: merger of the December 2026 AAA Financial Services index fund

- **Affected audience:** Holders of the merging scheme, Nippon India CRISIL - IBX AAA Financial Services - Dec 2026 Index Fund, and holders of the surviving scheme, Nippon India CRISIL-IBX Financial Services 9-12 Months Debt Index Fund.
- **Board approvals:** 22 and 23 July 2026.
- **SEBI no-objection:** 13 November 2026.
- **Public notice / announcement:** 27 November 2026 (Notice Cum Addendum No. 90).
- **Checked:** 28 November 2026.
- **Positive-consent / optional exit window:** 2 December–31 December 2026, ending at 3:00 pm IST on 31 December.
- **Merger effective date:** After close of business on 31 December 2026, or next business day if required.
- **Merging-scheme holders:** Positive consent is required to continue in the surviving scheme. If positive consent is not received by the stated deadline, the notice provides for redemption at the applicable NAV on the effective date, with proceeds remitted within three working days.
- **Surviving-scheme holders:** A separate 30-day exit option without exit load is available during the same window. No redemption by the deadline is treated as consent to the changes.
- **Systematic registrations:** For merging-scheme holders who positively consent and continue, existing SIP/STP/SWP registrations are stated to continue in the corresponding plan/option of the surviving scheme. Registrations should be cancelled if future transactions are not wanted.
- **Limitations:** Pledged/encumbered units have additional release conditions. The addendum includes a separate tax section; the article does not calculate an individual's tax result or infer a redemption value.

Primary sources:
- Notice Cum Addendum No. 90 (27 November 2026): https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/InvestorServices/Addenda/90-Merger-Crisil-IBX-Dec-26-with-NI-9-12-index.pdf
- Letter to unitholders (23 November 2026): https://mf.nipponindiaim.com/InvestorServices/Addenda/Letter-to-Unit-holder-Merger-Crisil-IBX-Dec-26-with-NI-9-12-index.pdf
